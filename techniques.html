01 / Experimental imaging

Living systems under observation.

Original explanatory diagram, not an experimental micrograph.

At Qatar University’s Biomedical Research Center I worked with zebrafish developmental and cardiovascular models, connecting specimen preparation to imaging acquisition and interpretation.

  • Zebrafish embryo and larval workflowsHusbandry, specimen preparation, phenotyping and developmental observations.
  • High-speed video microscopyTemporal observations of cardiac contractions, flow and vascular geometry in translucent zebrafish models.
  • Quantitative imaging and analysisImageJ and MicroZebraLab workflows, measurement quality and collaborative analysis.
  • Research infrastructureContribution to the establishment and standardization of biomedical imaging workflows at QU BRC.

02 / Cell and tissue mechanics

What physical stress does to cells.

Original conceptual illustration; experimental results are in the linked articles.

My work in pulmonary bioengineering used an experimental model of liquid-interface-induced epithelial injury to examine how physical stress affects cell survival and cytoskeletal organization.

  • Parallel-plate flow chamberFlow-induced stress wave generation and controlled epithelial exposure.
  • Cell preparation and assaysEpithelial monolayers, staining, live/dead assessment and fluorescence microscopy.
  • Mechanistic readoutsCell injury, cytoskeletal responses and experimental pharmacological protection.
  • Research communicationData interpretation, visual analysis and manuscript preparation.

04 / Pharmaceutics

Transporting a therapeutic where it matters.

Conceptual figure; ongoing MSc formulations are not presented as finalized results.

My MSc focuses on formulation and aerosolization questions in inhalable dexamethasone-loaded niosomes and proniosomes, bridging molecular composition, vesicular structure and pulmonary delivery.

  • Formulation designSurfactant/cholesterol composition calculations and vesicular carrier design.
  • Physicochemical assessmentDLS and FTIR workflows, with assay development and characterization planning.
  • Aerosol scienceNebulization, particle deposition and impactor-based evaluation in the thesis workflow.
  • Computational methodsPython-based formulation tools, quantitative data processing and mechanistic modelling concepts.
